Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need an account?

The Studio Plus local-library experience does not require an account. Music is stored locally; browser storage limits and clearing site data can affect persisted files.

Will removing tracks delete my music?

Removing imported tracks or playlists from Quera does not delete the original files on your device.

Is Atmosphere 2.0 a final release?

Atmosphere 2.0 is a beta sound enhancement. Its perceived effects vary depending on the track and output device.

Which version will I get?

The website showcases Studio Plus 2.2.0. The Microsoft Store, direct installer and Android links may offer different versions depending on deployment.
