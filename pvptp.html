Privacy First

Privacy Policy.

Your music belongs to you. Quera Music is designed with privacy, local processing, and a minimal-data approach at its core.

Last updated: August 25, 2026

01 — Introduction

Our Commitment to Privacy

Quera Music ("Quera Music", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Quera Music application, website, and related services.

Our application is primarily designed as an offline music player. We aim to collect as little information as reasonably necessary to provide the service.

02 — Information We Collect

Information We Collect

Quera Music is designed to operate without requiring you to create an account or provide personal information for normal offline music playback.

✦ Minimal Data by Design

Quera Music does not intentionally collect personal information merely because you use the music player.

Information you provide

If you voluntarily contact us, for example by email, we may receive the information that you choose to include in your message, such as your name, email address, and the contents of your request.

Local music files

Quera Music may access music files and folders on your device when you give the application the necessary permissions.

These files are used for local music playback and library functionality. Your local music collection is not intentionally uploaded to our servers for normal playback.

03 — Tracking & Analytics

No Unnecessary Tracking

Quera Music is designed to operate without advertising trackers, behavioral profiling, or unnecessary analytics within the music-player experience.

We do not intentionally build user profiles based on your listening activity.

We also do not intentionally collect information such as your listening history, playlists, or locally stored music library for advertising or behavioral tracking purposes.

04 — How Information Is Used

How Information Is Used

Any information that you voluntarily provide to us may be used only for legitimate purposes such as:

  • Responding to support or feedback requests.
  • Investigating technical problems you report.
  • Improving the application and user experience.
  • Communicating with you when necessary regarding your request.
  • Protecting the security and integrity of our services.

We do not use your local music collection or listening activity to create advertising profiles.

05 — Local Processing

Your Music, Your Device

Quera Music is built around local playback and processing. Features such as audio playback, equalization, and audio processing are intended to operate on your device.

This architecture helps reduce the amount of information that needs to leave your device.

✓ Local-first design

Your locally stored music files remain on your device unless you independently choose to share or transfer them using another service.

06 — Third-Party Services

Third-Party Services

Quera Music may be distributed through third-party platforms such as the Microsoft Store.

These platforms may independently collect and process information according to their own privacy policies. Such data collection is controlled by the respective platform and is not controlled by Quera Music.

If you download or install Quera Music through a third-party platform, we recommend reviewing that platform's privacy policy as well.

07 — Data Security

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information that is voluntarily provided to us.

However, no method of electronic transmission, storage, or internet communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

For this reason, while we work to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08 — Children's Privacy

Children's Privacy

Quera Music does not intentionally collect personal information from children through the normal use of its offline music-player functionality.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us by a child and should be removed, please contact us so that we can review the request.

09 — Your Rights

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information that you have voluntarily provided to us.

You may contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information that we may hold about you, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Because Quera Music is designed to minimize personal data collection, there may be little or no personal information associated with your use of the application.

10 — Data Retention

Data Retention

We retain voluntarily provided information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Local music files and music-library information stored on your device are controlled by you and remain subject to your device's own storage and operating-system controls.

11 — Policy Changes

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application, our practices, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.

We encourage you to review this page periodically for the latest information about our privacy practices.

12 — Contact

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Quera Music, you can contact us.

13 — Final Note

Privacy by Design

Quera Music was created with the idea that a music player should focus on playing your music—not collecting unnecessary information about you.

Our goal is to keep the experience fast, private, local, and focused on high-quality music playback.
